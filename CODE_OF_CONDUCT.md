# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Be direct, be kind, be useful. This is a small open standard project. Contributions and discussions should focus on the work.

Unacceptable behaviour: harassment, personal attacks, dismissiveness toward contributors regardless of experience level, or anything that makes people feel unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer at the contact listed in the repo. Issues will be handled privately and promptly.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum associated with Blueprint Protocol.
